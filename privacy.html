Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your data

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Username and email address
  • Encrypted password
  • Account creation date
  • Last login time

1.2 Scan Data

  • URLs submitted for analysis
  • Email content and headers
  • Messages analyzed
  • Analysis results and confidence scores

1.3 Usage Data

  • Time and date of scans
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Feature usage statistics

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Service Improvement

  • Training our AI and machine learning models
  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve features
  • Detecting and fixing technical issues
  • Enhancing threat detection algorithms

2.2 Personalization

  • Providing personalized scan history
  • Sending relevant security notifications
  • Customizing your dashboard experience

2.3 Communication

  • Responding to your inquiries and support requests
  • Sending important service updates
  • Notifying you of security incidents

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services for threat intelligence:

  • Google Safe Browsing - for URL reputation checks
  • VirusTotal - for malware detection
  • WHOIS databases - for domain age verification

These services receive only the URLs you submit for analysis and have their own privacy policies.

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process.

We do NOT sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

4. Data Security

4.1 Security Measures

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS
  • Data is stored in secure, encrypted databases
  • API keys are hashed and stored securely
  • Regular security audits and updates

4.2 Data Retention

  • Scan history is retained for as long as your account is active
  • You may delete your scan history at any time
  • Account deletion removes all associated data

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

  • View your scan history and account data
  • Update your account information
  • Delete individual scans or your entire history
  • Export your data in JSON format

5.2 Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time through your profile settings. This will permanently remove:

  • Your profile information
  • Scan history
  • API keys
  • Feedback and preferences

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
